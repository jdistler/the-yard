# Tea: a unicorn, a castle, and the cats

Built in `games/tea/tea.c`. The Yard lists it under Orb, blurb "tea with the cats," band 4+. `games/test.py` (run by `stress.sh`) plays the checks at the bottom of this note. The first build is on the cabinet, on the Yard row under Orb, as of the 2026-10-23 22:00 flash. The 2026-10-24 rebuild (new pictures and sound, same rules and lines) is on the PC only.

Audience is a four-year-old, in short turns, on the Partycade. She said: a unicorn is flying to a tea party at the princess castle, the party is full of kitty cats, and you play with them and take care of them. The game is that sentence, played with one hand if that is all she uses.

This is the little band in [CONTROLS.md](../../CONTROLS.md). Any stick direction does the one useful thing. Every button on the right does the care. There is no way to lose, no way to hurt a cat, and no clock that makes them sad while the cabinet is off.

## What a turn feels like

She walks up. A unicorn is already in the sky, wings moving, a small castle far ahead. If she only watches, the unicorn still flies, and the castle grows until the door opens. If she wiggles the stick, the unicorn leans and the clouds slide. If she presses any button on the right, the unicorn sparkles and hurries, still safe.

The door opens. Cats look out. One who remembers her from last time runs to meet the unicorn. Then they are at the table.

Three cats sit at a tea party. The unicorn stands in front, big, so she is who you are. A soft ring sits on one cat. Any push of the stick moves the ring to the next cat, whichever way she pushed. That cat leans in. Over its head is one big picture: a teacup, a ball of yarn, or a flower.

She presses the lower button, or either button beside it. The unicorn does the thing in the picture. The cat answers: a sip, a bat of the yarn, a twirl in a flower crown. A heart lights. Three hearts, and that cat is having a wonderful time. More presses still play. They do not empty the hearts.

When all three cats are glowing, the teapot sings, the cakes bounce, and the party cheers. She can keep playing. Nothing ends unless a grown-up holds 1-player and 2-player, which is how every game on this cabinet quits.

Next time she turns it on, the cats are wearing what she gave them. The party is new. They are glad she came back.

## The promise

* She cannot crash, miss, or make a cat unhappy.
* Watching is already play. The sky moves, the cats blink and sip, a yarn ball rolls and comes back.
* The first press on a cat succeeds. That success is the whole lesson.
* A turn is about two minutes if she fills every heart, and it is allowed to be ten seconds or ten minutes.
* Leaving is a goodbye. The gifts stay. Coming back is a reunion, not a rescue.
* She never has to read. A cream band at the bottom carries one short line for a grown-up to say out loud. The picture and the tune still work if nobody reads it.

## Who is at the table

She knows them by shape and tune. Their names show up in the line a grown-up reads, not as a label she has to decode.

| | Looks like | Asks with | Answers by | Tune |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pip** | Round orange cat, cream belly, small ears | A teacup | Sips, grows a milk mustache, eyes close, cup goes back to the table | Warm purr, a low soft note |
| **Moth** | Grey cat, darker stripes, tall ears | A yarn ball | Bats it, it rolls, it always rolls back, tail up | A bright chirp, two quick high notes |
| **Pearl** | White cat, gold bow, round cheeks | A flower | Wears it, twirls once, bow and flower both stay | Three little bells |
| **Nib** | A tiny kitten, cream with orange patches | Copies the friend you just played with | Does a smaller version of that answer, then bumps the unicorn's nose | The friend's tune, higher and shorter |

Nib is not at the first party. The save counts arrivals. On the third arrival, Nib is on a cushion at the end of the table and runs out with the others. Until then the table is Pip, Moth, and Pearl, always in that left-to-right order, so her hand learns the seats.

Each cat, while she is not pressing anything, blinks, looks at the unicorn, and once in a while does a tiny idle: Pip pretends to sip, Moth pats the yarn without losing it, Pearl smooths her bow. About every two seconds one of them does this, so the table is alive.

## Controls

One sentence: the stick visits the next friend, and any right-hand button takes care of the friend she is visiting.

| Input | In the sky | At the party |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Any stick direction | The unicorn leans that way. Clouds and the castle stay on course. She cannot fly away from the castle | The ring moves to the next cat. Left and up go one way, right and down the other. A diagonal, if the stick ever reports one, just moves once |
| `LOW`, `TL`, or `TR` | A sparkle and a little hurry. The flight still arrives on its own | Care, the picture over the focused cat. All three buttons do it. A mash does it once per press, and each press is a new delight |
| Hold a right-hand button | The hurry continues, wings flap faster, still no crash | The care repeats gently, about twice a second, with the cat answering each time, so holding is allowed and visible |
| `1P` + `2P` held | Quit, the cabinet rule | Quit. A grown-up's chord. She is not asked to do this |
| Volume, power | The cabinet's | The cabinet's |

The stick never has to be precise. Seats are huge. The ring snaps. She does not aim at a pixel.

There is no tool menu. The cat shows the picture of what would be lovely right now, and one press gives it. After three hearts the picture becomes a sparkle, and the press becomes play: Pip hums, Moth chases, Pearl curtsies. Same button. The toy changes. Her finger does not.

The title and the sky are the same place. The unicorn is already flying when the picture appears. The stick does not secretly move a body she cannot see.

## The three beats

### Sky

About fifteen seconds if she only watches. A held button can bring the castle in by about eight seconds. She cannot make it longer than about twenty: the castle arrives, because the party is the point and a four-year-old's flight should not become a wait.

On screen, top to bottom: blue sky, a few fat clouds, the unicorn large in the lower half, the castle small at the top and growing. A rainbow is the road, not a score. Stars along it light up as she gets closer, so progress is a picture.

Steer offset is a lean, a dozen pixels at most. The castle stays centered. Hitting the edge of the screen does nothing bad. The unicorn bumps a cloud and the cloud giggles (a soft pop, the cloud squishes and returns).

### The door

A short beat, under two seconds, and it plays itself. The door opens. Cats peek. If the save has a gift for a cat, that cat runs to the front and the unicorn dips her horn. Then the table is there. No button is required. If she presses one anyway, a sparkle happens and the beat does not break.

### The party

The castle fills the top. Towers, a flag, a window with a tiny crown. The table is a cream cloth across the middle, with a teapot, two cakes, and flowers that do not need to be clicked. Cats sit behind the cloth, big heads and paws visible. The unicorn stands in front, between the player and the table, so the avatar is the closest thing.

The focused cat is nearer, brighter, and leaning. The others stay visible and keep idling. Hearts float just above the focused cat: three small hearts, filled ones pink, empty ones a soft outline. The ask-picture sits above the hearts, one icon, large.

Care is immediate, on the same frame as the press:

1. The unicorn starts the motion (pour, toss, offer).
2. A note plays, the cat's tune.
3. The cat starts its answer.
4. A heart fills if fewer than three are filled.

The motion can finish over the next half-second. The heart is already lit on the press, so she never wonders whether it worked.

Hearts do not drain during the party or after it. Filling them is a hello, not a chore. When the last empty heart on the table fills, the cheer plays once: teapot steam makes a ring of sparkles, cakes hop, every cat does its answer together, the unicorn rears with her wings out. Then the asks become sparkles and play continues.

## What "take care of them" means

This is the Tamagotchi feeling with the punishment taken out. The cabinet's clock is stuck at the epoch, so a real-world hunger timer would be nonsense even if we wanted one. We do not want one.

What persists in `tea.sav`:

* How many times she has arrived. That is how Nib knows when to appear.
* The last gift each cat is wearing: none, flower, or a tiny teacup charm. Moth's yarn is always on the table, so Moth's souvenir is a ribbon on the yarn.

What does not persist, on purpose:

* Hearts. Each arrival starts empty, so there is a fresh party.
* Hunger, health, dirt, or mood. They are well every time the door opens.
* The clock. We never read it.

Saving happens when a gift is given and again on the cheer, through a temp file, fsync, rename, and a directory fsync, the same durability as a score. A power cut can lose the last gift. It cannot leave a cat "sick." If the file is missing or nonsense, the game starts as the first visit.

The file is a few short lines, for example:

```
visits 2
pip flower
moth ribbon
pearl flower
nib none
```

Unknown lines are ignored. A gift word outside that list is `none`. `visits` above 100000 is kept at 100000. The name `tea` is not one of the stock save names.

## Graphics

2D, upright, 384×512, the panel's exact ×2 scale. Drawn in software into one frame and uploaded once per frame, the path the stock emulators use. The Mali path can paint a room, and this game does not need it. Big flat shapes read better for her than a lit 3D scene, and the cabinet has not run a GL game yet.

Nearest-neighbor scaling. Thick outlines in soft ink `#3a2a44`, not pure black. Everything important is at least 32 pixels tall. The ask icon is 48×48. The unicorn is about 96 pixels tall on the ground and larger in the sky. Cats' heads are about 56 pixels. She should be able to tell them apart from a step away, and from her own height in front of the bartop.

Palette:

| | Hex | Used for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sky | `#8ec6ff` | The flight, and the air above the towers |
| Cloud, cloth, unicorn | `#fff6ea` | Clouds, tablecloth, unicorn body |
| Castle | `#f4b6d0` | Walls |
| Roof, filled heart | `#e86b9a` | Roofs, hearts, Pip's nose |
| Gold | `#f2c14e` | Horn, bow, cakes, star road |
| Mint | `#7ec8c3` | Window glass, one mane stripe |
| Lilac | `#b9a0e8` | One mane stripe, Nib's cushion |
| Tea | `#c9865a` | Tea in the cup and the pot |

| Grey | `#8a8f9c` | Moth |

The mane is four stripes in gold, rose, mint, and lilac, so the unicorn reads as rainbow without a thin spectrum. Wings are two simple shapes that swap vertical position for a flap. The horn is a single gold triangle.

As built (2026-10-24), the art is drawn in code with `games/common/gfx.c`, not loaded from an atlas, so there is no picture file to lose. Every figure is outlined toon shapes: each part is painted once in ink a little larger, then filled, so one soft outline wraps the whole body. Nothing is from a film, a book, or a product.

* The unicorn is seen from behind, so she is you: cream body, rainbow mane down her back, gold horn, lilac hooves, and wings of soft puffs that beat in the sky and fold at the table. Her wing beat is cut into eight steps and each is drawn once and reused.
* The sky is a fly-through: clouds start small near the castle and swell as they pass her. The rainbow road runs from her to the castle, and five stars on it light as she gets closer.
* At the door, two gold leaves swing open on warm light, the cats peek out one by one, and a cat wearing a gift runs out to her.
* At the party: the castle and bunting behind, a scalloped cloth, the teapot steaming, a cake, and Moth's yarn ball. The cats have ears with pink insides, eyes that blink and look at the unicorn, whiskers, a tail with a tip, and their gifts. A happy cat's eyes become arcs. The focused cat sits forward inside a soft gold ring. The ask picture floats in a bubble with a tail over her, above three hearts.
* Care, on the press frame: a heart fills and pops, the picture arcs from the unicorn to the cat, hearts drift up, and the cat answers (Pip sips and keeps a milk mustache, the yarn rolls away and back, Pearl twirls, Nib copies a friend). The cheer rains stars and hearts.

## Sound

The synth already in the other games. Sine or soft triangle, never a harsh square for her. Amplitude stays under the Keep beep. The cabinet's volume buttons still own loudness. We do not write `spk_volume`.

All tunes are major and pentatonic, slow, and short. Nothing in a minor key, nothing that sounds like an alarm.

| Moment | What she hears |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sky, the bed | C5, E5, G5, repeating, quiet, one note every half second |
| Lean | One quiet G5 |
| Cloud bump | A soft A5 pop |
| Hurry | The bed, a little faster |
| Door, a cat who remembers | That cat's tune, once |
| Pip's care | A low purr around G3 under a single E5 |
| Moth's care | G5 then C6, short |
| Pearl's care | C6, E6, G6, bell-like, quiet |
| Nib's care | Whichever of those, up a fifth, fewer notes |
| Heart fills | The cat's tune, with a tiny C6 on top the first time that heart lights |
| Cheer | C5, E5, G5, C6, once, still gentle |
| Quit | One falling G5, E5, so the goodbye is a sound too |

Two sounds can overlap: the bed and one care. A third note waits. The speaker stays a room, not a pile.

As built (2026-10-24) the synth has more voices, so a care's notes can ring over each other for a moment, and the party has its own very quiet music-box bed in place of silence. Everything stays sine and triangle and major, and the output is capped at 3000 of 32767 (`sfx_limit`), under the Keep beep. Pip's purr is a low triangle with a slow wobble.

Holding the button retriggers the care no faster than twice a second, so a clamped finger does not machine-gun the speaker.

## The line you read

The cabinet has an 8×8 font and no outline typeface. One line of that font, drawn at ×2, fits 24 characters across the 384-wide picture. On the glass that is about a third of an inch tall, which a grown-up at the bartop can read. The line sits in a cream band along the bottom, ink on `#fff6ea`, and the unicorn's hooves stop above it. Her eyes stay on the cats. Yours can drop to the band.

The line is narration, the sentence you would say next. It is not a control hint. She already has the picture for that. When the moment changes, the line changes on the same frame, so you are never reading the previous beat.

It rests on the current moment, and a reaction borrows it for about two seconds, then the resting line comes back:

| Moment | The line |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sky, watching | `She flies to the castle.` |
| Sky, she leaned | `She leans in the clouds.` |
| Sky, she hurried | `The party is waiting.` |
| Door, first visit | `They saved her a seat.` |
| Door, a cat kept a gift | `Pearl kept her flower.` |
| Nib's first peek | `A kitten came to play.` |
| Ring on Pip, hearts open | `Pip would love some tea.` |
| Ring on Moth, hearts open | `Moth wants the yarn.` |
| Ring on Pearl, hearts open | `Pearl wants a flower.` |
| Ring on Nib, hearts open | `Nib copies a friend.` |
| Just poured for Pip | `Pip sipped her tea.` |
| Just played with Moth | `The yarn rolled back.` |
| Just crowned Pearl | `Pearl wears a flower.` |
| Just played with Nib | `Nib bumped her nose.` |
| That cat's third heart | `Pip is so happy.` |
| The cheer | `The whole table glows.` |
| After the cheer, still playing | `Play on. They love it.` |
| Goodbye | `They will keep the gifts.` |

The reunion and "so happy" lines use whichever cat it actually is. Pearl's flower line becomes `Moth kept a ribbon.` or `Pip kept a teacup.` when that is the cat who ran to the door. If more than one cat is wearing a gift, the leftmost one runs, and the line names that one.

Idle blinks and sips do not change the line. You would be reading every blink. The band speaks when she does something, when the castle arrives, and when the table cheers.

## Reactions, listed

Every press has an answer. There is no wrong press.

* Stick in the sky: lean, one note, clouds drift the other way a few pixels.
* Button in the sky: sparkles on the horn, wings speed up, the castle eases closer.
* Stick into a cloud: the cloud squishes, pops, returns. No damage.
* Stick at the party: the ring snaps to the next cat, that cat leans, the previous cat sits back and smiles. The lean is the whole UI for "this one."
* Button on a cat with empty hearts: the care animation, the tune, a heart fills.
* Button on a cat whose hearts are full: the play animation, the tune, sparkles, hearts stay full.
* Button while a care is still playing: the new care starts. We do not queue a puzzle of inputs. Her latest press is the one that matters.
* Idle, two seconds: some cat blinks or sips or pats. The unicorn's wings keep a slow flap in the sky, and on the ground she shifts her hooves.
* Arrival with a saved gift: that cat is drawn wearing it from the first frame of the door, and runs forward.
* Third arrival: Nib is in the peek, then on the cushion.
* Cheer: everyone delights once. Then play continues on sparkle icons.
* Quit: the falling goodbye notes, gifts already saved, process exits, the stock menu returns.

## What this game will not do

These are decisions, so a later build does not "improve" them back in.

* No sickness, hunger, dirt, scolding, or a cat that cries because she left.
* No game over, no score to beat, no timer counting down.
* No reading, no menu of tools, no mode that changes what the stick means.
* No second player with their own stick. A grown-up can sit with her and press the button while she holds the stick. That split already fits the panel.
* No 3D, no video cutscene, no recorded music file. The pictures and the synth are the whole production, and they fit in the space `data` has left.

## How we will know it works

`games/test.py` plays this headless, on the PC (`stress.sh`):

* The sky shows the unicorn without a button.
* A scripted stick lean moves her and does not lose the castle.
* Arrival happens even with no input.
* At the table, a stick press moves the ring, and `LOW` fills a heart that is visible in a screenshot on that same frame.
* Nine cares (three cats, three hearts) reach the cheer.
* `tea.sav` then contains the gifts, and a second launch draws those gifts on the cats at the door.
* A broken save opens as the first visit.
* `TL` and `TR` also fill a heart.
* 1-player and 2-player still quit.
* A button held from the Yard into the game does not fire a pile of cares on the first frames. The sky hurry is fine. The party care waits for a new press after arrival.

The Yard row is under Orb. Keep and Lamp stay where they were. Nothing here has been flashed.

## What we kept

The seed is hers. These are the choices the build follows:

1. **Flight is short and cannot fail.** She always gets to the party. A held button only makes the castle arrive sooner.
2. **Any direction picks the next cat. Any right-hand button does the care.** She does not choose tea versus yarn versus flower. The cat asks, in a picture.
3. **Hearts refill as a hello every visit. Gifts stay.** Nib joins at the third arrival.
4. **Pip, Moth, and Pearl** are the three cats, orange-and-tea, grey-and-yarn, white-and-flower. Their names are in the line you read. She still plays from the pictures if you say nothing.
5. **Big flat 2D**, pink castle, cream unicorn, thick outlines. Not the 3D well.
6. **A grown-up quits** with 1-player and 2-player. She is never asked to.

Those six are what the PC build plays. A cabinet install is a later flash, and it needs a fresh dump and a written block list first.
