# Summer (`games/love/home/summer.lua`, `tools/art/summer.py`)

## Round 2 (2026-11-06): bigger, closer, and an ending for every scene

The stories were already there (the cliff-jumping contest, the dive and the belly-flop, the race, the brain
freeze, the rapids, the rainbow slide); the review found them told too small (the cast at radius 18-30, about
40-60 px on the screen) and several scenes ending in a long still picture.

### Done

* **Bigger**: the cast 30% bigger in every scene; Barton Springs' race and floats, the camera in on the race.
* **Snow cones**: the cones made big at the window (ice flakes flying, a thick syrup stream), a hop when served,
  the servings quicker; Pac-Man's own is a tower of every flavour, scoop on scoop, the ghosts gathered and
  hopping, the drips at 104°, the lean (TIMBERRR!), and down it goes into his mouth.
* **Tubing**: the take-out is a rope swing off a big oak: a dive, a flip, a belly-flop, a twirl, the cannonball.
* **The finale**: each off the pier in their own style; fireworks over the lake.
* **Songs**: the lake song a speedboat strain and a lift (33 -> 66 s), the bossa a C strain (30 -> 60 s).
